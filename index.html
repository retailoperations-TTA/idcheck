            Scanner setup
            
      
            The scanner has to be a 2D imager that reads PDF417, the wide barcode on the back of licenses. It types like a keyboard, so this page must stay open and clicked.
            
      
            Passports: the scanner also needs OCR-B turned on. On a Zebra scanner, scan Enable OCR-B, then OCR-B Passport (or ICAO Travel Documents, if listed) from the OCR Programming chapter of its Product Reference Guide.
